# Gravewave: Midnight Parade

## Product
A browser-playable, portrait-first anime squad shooter with roguelike runs. A complete playable game comes before native packaging or monetization. Static assets and TypeScript simulation are kept portable to an eventual iOS wrapper.

## Player and promise
For players who want immediate arcade action, stylish original anime heroines, satisfying squad growth, and a different build on each run. Start playing without an account, install, payment, or energy timer. Touch drag and keyboard controls must both work.

## Core experience
Lead a kawaii-goth heroine and her ghost-fan squad across moonlit cathedral bridges through a cursed carnival. Auto-fire at possessed plushies and cursed amplifiers, recruit friendly ghosts at gates, and choose risky side routes. Telegraph boss attacks; reward dodging and positioning. Between encounters, choose one of three upgrades and discover synergistic builds. Complete three distinct stages or replay an escalating survival mode. Charge an Encore ultimate through combat.

## Character and collection layer
Three original adult heroines have distinct weapons, personalities, signature bursts, and cosmetic outfits. Skippable animated anime sequences establish the mission, introduce bosses, and celebrate victories. Persistent earned currency unlocks heroes and cosmetics through a clear preview/buy/equip shop. Outfits do not sell combat advantages. No real-money checkout, loot boxes, paid random pulls, login streaks, or premium currency in this version.

## Visual direction
Playful kawaii-goth Japanese anime: cute expressive adult faces, sparkling eyes, mischievous smiles, crisp cel shading, black lace and velvet, silver jewelry, platform boots, and glamorous stage outfits. Wuthering Waves is a high-level reference for polish and appeal, not copied designs. A violet midnight sea, ghost-blue lanterns, black cathedral bridges, moon-white text, and crimson danger cues create a distinctive haunted carnival. Ghost fans, possessed plushies, coffin carriages, and stained-glass bosses make the theme part of play rather than a black recolor. Keep combat readable on a small phone.

## Quality bar
A polished first play, not a mock dashboard: immediate controls, clear goals, responsive hits, meaningful choices, fair failure, instant retry, a complete victory path, persistent cosmetics, and an actually usable shop. Respect reduced motion, pause when hidden, provide sound controls and keyboard-visible focus, and make the game usable in portrait and desktop layouts.

## Boundaries
Original characters, artwork, names, dialogue, and audio. No third-party game art, recording, branding, or music is shipped. No backend, accounts, analytics, advertising, payment integration, or native build is claimed. Local saves are browser-local and not secure entitlements; paid products would require a new trusted purchase/entitlement design.

## Validation
Unit tests prove simulation, run choices, reward accounting, and save/shop behavior. A real browser must prove touch/keyboard play, campaign and survival, cinematics, cosmetics, pause/retry, persistence, responsive layouts, and static subpath loading. Public deployment requires an explicit publication gate separate from building the game.
